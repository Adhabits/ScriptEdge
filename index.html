Meet the Founder

Mudasir Rashid

Founder & CEO, ScriptEdge

From Jammu & Kashmir to building a growing affiliate marketing ecosystem across India, Mudasir Rashid's journey is driven by one clear vision: helping affiliate marketers become more skilled, confident, and effective at selling.

With 4+ years in the national and international affiliate marketing space, Mudasir has worked closely with the challenges affiliate marketers face every day — from generating quality leads and building buyer trust to handling sales conversations and converting potential customers into actual buyers.

His journey has involved guiding thousands of affiliate marketers and contributing to ₹55 Lakh+ in documented business generation through the affiliate marketing ecosystem.

Why he founded ScriptEdge

Mudasir noticed a common problem: having a product, leads, and basic knowledge is not always enough to generate consistent sales. Many marketers struggle with communication, buyer psychology, objection handling, follow-ups, and building trust with potential customers.

That is where ScriptEdge was born — to bridge the gap between knowing affiliate marketing and actually executing it effectively, through structured learning resources, practical sales guides, communication frameworks, expert-led sessions, and mentorship.

His vision

Successful affiliate marketing is not just about sharing links or promoting products. It is about understanding people, communicating value, building trust, and developing the right skills through consistent execution.

Better Skills. Better Conversations. Better Execution.— Mudasir Rashid, Founder & CEO of ScriptEdge
